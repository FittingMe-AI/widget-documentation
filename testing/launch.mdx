Give each open item an owner.

## Configuration

- [ ] Production access includes try-on.
- [ ] The key matches the retailer and environment.
- [ ] The key authorises every production hostname, `www` included.
- [ ] FittingMe approved framing for every production origin.
- [ ] No `localhost` key or placeholder remains.
- [ ] Garment images meet the [image URL requirements](/integration/products#image-url-requirements).
- [ ] The production CSP allows the loader and iframe.

## Shopper experience

- [ ] A complete try-on works on representative products.
- [ ] The phone handoff works on real devices.
- [ ] Close and reopen restore scrolling and focus.
- [ ] Back closes the drawer for each opening method you ship.
- [ ] Product changes and SPA navigation work.
- [ ] Failures and retries leave shopping usable.

## Data and measurement

- [ ] The privacy owner reviewed consent, and analytics respects it.
- [ ] Reports do not count completion as unique generations.
- [ ] Logs exclude keys and photos.

## Release and recovery

- [ ] You can disable or remove the integration.
- [ ] You can restore the previous build.
- [ ] An owner checks real traffic after release.
- [ ] The team knows the [support route](/troubleshooting/support).

## Verify after enabling

Open a public product page in a fresh session and complete the journey. Check your monitoring for CSP errors and access rejections.

If shopping breaks, disable the entry point or remove the integration. Revoke a key only if every installation that uses it must stop. See [update, disable, or remove](/maintenance/updates).
