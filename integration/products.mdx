Each embed needs `data-product-id` and `data-garment-image-urls`. FittingMe uses its catalogue photos first and your page photos as a fallback. All views show the same garment and colour. `data-garment-image-url` is a deprecated one-photo alias: if both attributes are present, the plural wins.

## Accepted garment categories

**Clothing:** sweatshirts, jackets, coats, polo shirts, shirts, jumpers, cardigans, trousers, jeans, T-shirts, vest tops, shorts, joggers, tracksuits, sportswear, suits and sets.

**Footwear:** trainers, smart shoes, boots, sports shoes, sandals, mules, espadrilles, flip-flops and slippers.

Ask FittingMe before you add a product outside these categories.

## Supply garment views

1. Use one stable product identifier on the page, in the embed and in your analytics.
2. Select views of that exact product and colour, with a front view first.
3. Check each URL against the requirements below.
4. Set `data-product-id` and `data-garment-image-urls` before the v5 script loads. See the [quickstart](/start/quickstart).
5. Generate a result and check the garment.

### Image URL requirements

- Absolute HTTPS URL with a hostname, at most 1,024 characters.
- JPEG or PNG, at most 20 MiB and 8,192 pixels per side.
- At most 3 redirects.
- No user name, password, `#` fragment, whitespace or control characters.
- Available without cookies or sign-in.
- On a public host. The loader skips private addresses, `localhost` and hosts that end in `.local` or `.internal`.
- The encoded list has at most 6,144 characters. A longer list sends no page photo.

Configure your image CDN to always serve JPEG or PNG: automatic WebP delivery fails. A bad image fails only after the shopper selects **Start**.

The attribute takes one plain URL or a JSON array of URLs. FittingMe trims the URLs, removes duplicates in order and skips invalid members. Malformed JSON logs a console error and sends no page photo, but catalogue photos still apply.

## Prepare catalogue imagery

1. Arrange catalogue onboarding with FittingMe. Agree the product identifiers and source images.
2. Get confirmation that the products are ready for try-on.
3. Put the agreed identifier in `data-product-id`.
4. Test one eligible product and one excluded product.

## When Try-On is unavailable

FittingMe never offers Try-On for swimwear, lingerie, underwear, nightwear and children's products. Images from your page do not change this. A product with a missing or unknown category stays eligible. For an excluded product, the [bootstrap outcome](/reference/events#bootstrap-outcome) is `ineligible`: hide your trigger.

To change the product on a page, see [single-page and dynamic storefronts](/integration/react).
