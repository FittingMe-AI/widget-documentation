The web team of the retailer installs, configures, tests, launches and troubleshoots the integration, and contacts support. The data team reads [Page Events](/reference/events) and [Delivered Events](/reference/delivered-events).

FittingMe provides the try-on drawer, consent, photo, generation and result. Start with one product and the loader, a JavaScript file that creates an isolated iframe.

## The shopper experience

1. The shopper opens try-on from a product page.
2. FittingMe asks for consent and a photo. On a computer, the shopper can use the phone handoff.
3. FittingMe generates and shows the try-on result.
4. The shopper closes the drawer and keeps shopping.

## Who owns what

| Your team | FittingMe |
| --- | --- |
| Storefront and layout | Loader, widget and services |
| Product identifier and garment image | Photo capture and generation |
| Product, cart and checkout | Consent and journey state |
| Security policy and analytics consent | Page Events and errors |
| Tests on your storefront | Access and catalogue onboarding |
