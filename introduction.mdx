FittingMe lets a shopper supply a photo and see a generated image of themselves wearing a garment. You place a **Journey Embed** on a product page. It contains the Try-On interface and opens it in a drawer. Each embed has one product context and its own session.

## Choose your integration

| Approach | What you build | What FittingMe handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Provided button](/quickstarts/provided-button) | A container and a configured loader script | The Journey Trigger, iframe, drawer coordination and browser messaging |
| [Your own button](/quickstarts/custom-button) — recommended for custom buttons | Your button, boot-state handling and focus return | The same Try-On interface, plus the loader's lifecycle and messaging |
| [Direct fittingme/3](/quickstarts/direct-protocol) — advanced | The iframe host, validated protocol, fullscreen layout, history, scroll lock, focus return and teardown | The interface inside the iframe and its service requests |

A custom button opens the FittingMe interface. It does not replace the interface with a retailer-built upload or result screen. Use the loader unless you need to own the iframe host itself.

## Get to your first try-on

1. [Obtain access and configure a key](/start/access-keys-domains). FittingMe provides initial access; your retailer admin manages keys and domains in the Retailer Portal.
2. [Prepare a product identifier and garment image URL](/start/first-product).
3. Copy one complete quickstart, replace its placeholders, and serve it from an authorized HTTP or HTTPS origin.
4. Open the drawer, complete the consent steps, supply a test photo and generate a try-on. Close the drawer and verify the observed events.
5. Work through the [go-live checklist](/testing/go-live) on your actual storefront.

All three quickstarts use a garment image you supply. They do not need a catalogue import. If FittingMe has prepared your catalogue, follow [Use a prepared catalogue](/guides/prepared-catalogue) instead.

[Configuration](/reference/configuration), the [loader API](/reference/loader-api), the [direct protocol](/reference/fittingme-3) and [events](/reference/events) define the integration contract. Tracking recipes build on these events; FittingMe does not emit your store's purchases.
