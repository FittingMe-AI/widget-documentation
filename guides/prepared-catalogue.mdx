---
title: "Use a prepared catalogue"
description: "Hand product data to FittingMe, then embed prepared products through the loader."
---

Catalogue preparation is FittingMe-assisted. Your team supplies product data; FittingMe prepares it and confirms which product identifiers can serve Try-On. This walkthrough uses only the loader integration.

## Prepare the handoff

Agree the delivery format and update cadence with your FittingMe contact. The table below describes the information to supply; it is not an import endpoint or a mandatory file schema.

| Information | What to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Product identifier | Stable store identifier for each garment/color context; the same value the product page will pass as `data-product-id` |
| Garment image URL | Direct, publicly reachable HTTPS image representing that context |
| Product name | A readable label to review the mapping |
| Brand and category | Your source values, so FittingMe can map them to its catalogue vocabulary |
| Product/color relationship | How the page's selected color maps to its identifier and image |
| Availability and removals | Which items should be offered and how you will communicate updates or removals |
| Storefront URL | A representative page where the mapping can be checked |

For example, send a small review sample first:

```csv
product_id,name,color,brand,category,garment_image_url,storefront_url
YOUR_NAVY_PRODUCT_ID,Example shirt,Navy,YOUR_BRAND,YOUR_CATEGORY,https://images.example.com/navy-shirt.jpg,https://shop.example.com/shirt
YOUR_WHITE_PRODUCT_ID,Example shirt,White,YOUR_BRAND,YOUR_CATEGORY,https://images.example.com/white-shirt.jpg,https://shop.example.com/shirt
```

Do not invent a FittingMe model key or an eligibility flag. FittingMe owns the prepared mapping and confirms availability. The supplied URLs must follow the [image requirements](/start/first-product#supply-a-garment-image).

## Confirm readiness

Before integration, obtain confirmation of:

- The target environment and retailer.
- The exact prepared product identifiers and garment/color mapping.
- Usable imagery and Try-On availability.
- How subsequent imagery changes and removals will be handed over.

A sent spreadsheet is not proof that its products are ready.

Your retailer admin separately configures [keys and domains](/start/access-keys-domains). There is no catalogue management screen to use as part of this walkthrough.

## Place your first prepared product

1. Start from the complete [provided-button example](/quickstarts/provided-button).
2. Replace its product identifier with one confirmed by FittingMe.
3. **Remove** the `data-garment-image-url` attribute. The loader chooses the appropriate protocol; do not force a protocol version.
4. Keep the quickstart's subscription and cleanup code.

Its essential mount becomes:

```html
<div id="product-tryon"></div>
<script src="https://widget.fittingme.ai/v2/fittingme-loader.js"
  data-api-base="https://api.fittingme.ai"
  data-retailer-key="YOUR_PUBLISHABLE_KEY"
  data-product-id="YOUR_PREPARED_PRODUCT_ID"
  data-journey="tryon"
  data-trigger-mode="encart"
  data-locale="en"
  data-container="#product-tryon"></script>
```

To use your own trigger, apply the same omission to the [custom-button example](/quickstarts/custom-button); keep `data-trigger-mode="hôte"` and its focus-return handler.

### Verify prepared products

1. Open the page from an authorized origin. Expect `amorçage` with `issue: "éligible"`, then the trigger becomes usable.
2. Complete a Try-On journey and verify the garment against the prepared product/color.
3. Check a second prepared item.
4. Check a product confirmed unavailable. It must not offer an active Try-On trigger.

## Diagnose the handoff

An unknown identifier, an explicit product exclusion, missing usable prepared imagery or missing entitlement can make catalogue Try-On unavailable. The public event does not identify which reason applies; do not infer the cause from zero height.

1. Check the exact identifier and environment against the handoff.
2. Have FittingMe verify the prepared mapping.

A supplied image would override catalogue imagery; accidentally leaving that attribute in your template means you are no longer testing catalogue resolution. For product selection and SPA changes, follow [Product and image changes](/guides/product-changes).
