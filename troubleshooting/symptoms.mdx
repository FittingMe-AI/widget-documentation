Do not share keys, photos or capture links.

## Nothing appears

1. In Network, find `fittingme-loader.js`. If it is missing, check the URL and CSP.
2. Create the container before the script, outside hidden or unmounted components.
3. Read the console for an unknown `data-*` attribute or malformed `data-garment-image-urls`. Some console messages are in French.
4. If both image attributes are set, the plural attribute wins. It needs the `/v5/` loader.
5. If the console shows a `frame-ancestors` refusal, ask FittingMe to approve your origin.
6. Inspect the state:

```javascript
const container = document.getElementById('fittingme-widget-container');
const fm = window.FittingMe;
const state = fm?.resolve(container);
console.table({
  loaderAvailable: Boolean(fm),
  embedMounted: Boolean(state),
  bootstrapOutcome: state?.outcome ?? 'not reported',
  drawerOpen: state?.drawer_open ?? false
});
```

`undefined` means no live embed, `ineligible` means no journey and `indeterminate` means unknown availability. Some categories never offer try-on; see [when try-on is unavailable](/integration/products#when-try-on-is-unavailable).

In `host` trigger mode, FittingMe shows no button, so render your own.

## Authentication or domain access fails

1. Check that the key matches the retailer and environment.
2. Check that the key is not revoked.
3. Compare the hostname with the key's domains, `www` included.
4. For local tests, use a `localhost` key on an HTTP `localhost` URL.
5. If access is forbidden with a valid key, ask FittingMe to check the entitlement.

The domain check runs when the session opens. See [error codes](/reference/errors).

## The image works on the page but generation fails

FittingMe's servers fetch the image without cookies or sign-in. It must be JPEG or PNG, at most 20 MiB, with at most three redirects; see [image URL requirements](/integration/products#image-url-requirements).

## The wrong garment appears

An attribute edit after the script runs does not update the iframe. On a product or colour change, close and [replace the embed](/integration/react). If the page attributes are right, ask FittingMe to check the product-to-image mapping.

## The drawer is clipped or refuses to open

An ancestor with `transform` or `filter` breaks the fixed iframe. So does a `will-change` of transform, filter, perspective or contain, or a `contain` of paint, layout, strict or content. The loader then refuses to open with `fullscreen_unavailable`. Its console message, in French, names the ancestor and the property. Move the container, or remove the property.

## A custom button does nothing

- Enable the button after `bootstrap` with `outcome === 'eligible'`.
- Pass the container element to `open()`, not its ID.
- Read the returned [command verdict](/reference/javascript#command-verdicts).
- Wait for `opened`. `accepted` alone does not prove that the drawer appeared.

## Product navigation leaves duplicates or broken scrolling

Do not remove a container after `destroy()` returns `drawer_open`. A full page reload restores a stranded page. See [close before replacing](/integration/react#close-before-replacing).

## Browser Back leaves the drawer open

Reproduce with a slow network and rapid navigation, then see [single-page and dynamic storefronts](/integration/react).

## A photo is refused

If the provider refuses the photo after Start, the drawer offers to change the photo or return to the product. See [errors and recovery](/reference/errors#photo-refusal-and-service-failure).

## Photo handoff or generation does not finish

Keep the product page open. Retry with a fresh QR code. If it still fails, collect the [support details](/troubleshooting/support).

## Analytics events are missing or duplicated

Subscribe before interaction and filter by `event.embed`; see the [tracking guide](/integration/analytics).
