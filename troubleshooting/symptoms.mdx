Do not share keys, photos or capture links.

## Nothing appears

1. In Network, find `fittingme-loader.js`. If it is missing, check the URL and CSP.
2. Create the container before the script, outside hidden or unmounted components.
3. Read the console for an unknown `data-*` attribute or a malformed `data-garment-image-urls`. Some console messages are in French.
4. If both image attributes are set, the plural attribute takes priority. It needs the `/v5/` loader.
5. If the console shows a `frame-ancestors` refusal, first check that the key is the full, active key of this environment. Then ask FittingMe to check that the page's hostname and the hostname of each parent frame are on the key's domains. Reload after a change. See [storefront framing](/start/prerequisites#check-storefront-framing).
6. If FittingMe does not know the product, a page photo must be on one of the key's image hosts. Otherwise the bootstrap outcome is `ineligible` and no button shows.
7. Inspect the state:

```javascript
const container = document.getElementById('fittingme-widget-container');
const fm = window.FittingMe;
const state = fm?.resolve(container);
console.table({
  loaderAvailable: Boolean(fm),
  embedMounted: Boolean(state),
  bootstrapOutcome: state?.outcome ?? 'not reported',
  drawerOpen: state?.drawer_open ?? false
});
```

`undefined` means there is no live embed, `ineligible` means there is no journey, and `indeterminate` means availability is unknown. Some categories never offer try-on; see [when try-on is unavailable](/integration/products#when-try-on-is-unavailable).

In `host` trigger mode, FittingMe shows no button, so render your own.

## Authentication or domain access fails

1. Check that the key matches the retailer and environment.
2. Ask FittingMe to check that the key is not revoked.
3. Ask FittingMe to compare the hostname with the key's domains, including `www`.
4. For local tests, use a local-only key that lists only `localhost`, on an HTTP `localhost` URL.
5. If access is forbidden with a valid key, ask FittingMe to check the entitlement.

The browser checks the key's domains when the widget loads, and the API checks them again when the session opens. See [error codes](/reference/errors).

## The image works on the page but generation fails

FittingMe's servers fetch the image without cookies or sign-in. It must be JPEG or PNG, at most 20 MiB, with at most three redirects; see [image URL requirements](/integration/products#image-url-requirements).

## The wrong garment appears

Editing an attribute after the script runs does not update the iframe. On a product or colour change, close and [replace the embed](/integration/react). If the page attributes are right, ask FittingMe to check the product-to-image mapping.

## The drawer is clipped or refuses to open

An ancestor with `transform` or `filter` breaks the fixed iframe. So does a `will-change` of transform, filter, perspective or contain, or a `contain` of paint, layout, strict or content. The loader then refuses to open with `fullscreen_unavailable`. Its console message, in French, names the ancestor and the property. Move the container, or remove the property.

## A custom button does nothing

- Enable the button after `bootstrap` with `outcome === 'eligible'`.
- Pass the container element to `open()`, not its ID.
- Read the returned [command verdict](/reference/javascript#command-verdicts).
- Wait for `opened`. `accepted` alone does not prove that the drawer appeared.

## Product navigation leaves duplicates or broken scrolling

Do not remove a container after `destroy()` returns `drawer_open`. A full page reload restores a stranded page. See [close before replacing](/integration/react#close-before-replacing).

## Browser Back leaves the drawer open

Reproduce with a slow network and rapid navigation, then see [single-page and dynamic storefronts](/integration/react).

## A photo is refused

| Shopper sees | Meaning and next action |
| --- | --- |
| Refusal before Start | No person, a clearly young child, explicit nudity, or a reading blocked by the model. Choose another photo; the preview offers no Start. |
| Advice about several people, framing, blur or darkness | Continue with this photo or choose another. With several people, the advice does not say who will be dressed. |
| No message after a slow or unavailable reading | The journey continues. This does not certify the photo. |
| Refusal after Start | Change the photo or return to the product. |

During handoff, the refusal shows on the phone and the desktop drawer follows.

The widget owns these controls. Do not recreate these checks in your storefront. See [errors and recovery](/reference/errors#photo-refusal-and-service-failure).

## Photo handoff or generation does not finish

Keep the product page open. Retry with a fresh QR code. If it still fails, collect the [support details](/troubleshooting/support).

## Analytics events are missing or duplicated

Subscribe before interaction and filter by `event.embed`; see the [tracking guide](/integration/analytics).
