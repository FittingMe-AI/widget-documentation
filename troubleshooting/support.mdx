Use your FittingMe contact, or email [contact@fittingme.ai](mailto:contact@fittingme.ai). Contact FittingMe for access, entitlement, framing approval, catalogue onboarding or a service failure.

## Prepare a useful report

1. The retailer, environment and hostname.
2. The date, time and time zone.
3. The browser, operating system and device.
4. The garment image URLs.
5. The steps, the expected result and the actual result.
6. The bootstrap outcome or command verdict, the HTTP status and the error `code`.
7. The scope: one product, one browser or all attempts.

## Redact before sharing

Remove keys, cookies, session identifiers, capture or QR URLs, photos, result URLs and personal data. A `type` URI is an identifier, not a page: send the `code`.

## Report the impact clearly

Tell FittingMe if cart or checkout is affected. If shoppers are blocked, [disable the entry point](/maintenance/updates#disable-the-entry-point).
